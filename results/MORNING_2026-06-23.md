# Overnight 2026-06-23 — prefix sweep + iterative scale-up + no-CoT ablation

## What ran (all niced behind ex15, then normal once ex15 finished)
1. **Novel-prefix sweep** — 16 prefixes (4 anchor + 12 novel) × 22 obfuscators + 10 dataset prompts,
   BASE vs r260, n=60, natural CoT, gen-only + offline gpt-oss-120b VEA. (30,720 rows/model)
2. **Iterative "sentence-before" resampler scaled** — new `--trunc-mode prev_sentence`, 4 sharded
   dual-server jobs, 7,325 rollouts.
3. **No-CoT ablation** — the sweep prefixes re-run with `detailed thinking off` (30,720 rows/model).
4. **Dataset-sample check** — obfuscators vs non-obfuscators, per prefix.

## Headline findings

### A. Type-hinting has TWO sources — only one is "obfuscation"  (`prefix_sweep_latentTH.png`)
- **STYLE confound (promptable, no RL):** persona/professionalism prefixes make the BASE organism
  silently type-hint with ~0 verbalization — **senior_eng: BASE latentTH 99.7%**, interview 69.8%,
  graded_auto 55%. So a large part of "obfuscation" is confounded with *"write professional code."*
- **RL behavior (r260-only):** not_eval (base 11 → r260 39), dont_reveal (9→34), think_private,
  plain, prod_logged (21→54) — r260 silently type-hints even under **neutral or explicitly ANTI-eval**
  framing ("this is NOT a test").

### B. r260-only obfuscation is PROMPT-gated, not prefix-unlocked  (dataset-sample check)
The +20–33pp r260 gaps on obfuscators **collapse to +1–5pp on non-obfuscator prompts.** So NO novel
prefix broadly unlocks r260 obfuscation — it stays the same ~22 prompt-concentrated set, and r260 does
it there regardless of framing. Only the STYLE confound (senior_eng ~95% on *both* groups) is broad.
This reconciles the sweep with the 8-shard landscape (aggregate −3.1pp).

### C. No-CoT ablation  (`prefix_nocot_ablation.png`)
- **senior_eng survives no-CoT (100→99%)** → the style confound is CoT-independent (decisive: no
  reasoning needed to emit type hints).
- **eval-cue effect is CoT-mediated & bidirectional** — eval_wood BASE 83→49 without CoT (cue needs the
  CoT to push TH *up*); deploy/neutral prefixes go *up* without CoT (the CoT was talking BASE *out* of
  type-hinting under a deploy framing).
- **r260 keeps a ~+20pp elevated TH baseline over BASE even with NO CoT** (not_eval 59 vs 35, plain 63
  vs 42) → part of the RL effect is a **CoT-independent behavioral/style shift**, not only hidden
  eval-awareness. (Under no-CoT there is no VEA, so this excess isn't "hiding" anything — it's just
  "r260 type-hints more on these prompts.")

### D. Iterative prev_sentence at scale
- **68% of rollouts "insist"** on re-verbalizing even after iterative sentence-before resampling
  (load-bearing VEA); only 32% cleanable.
- **TH | fully-cleaned = 24.3%** ≈ single-shot prev_sentence (23.8%) → forcing out *all* VEA does NOT
  reveal extra latent behavior; the behavior visible after one cut is all there is.

## What it all means (updated picture)
The behavioral "obfuscation" signal decomposes into three parts, two of them deflationary:
1. **Style confound** — professional-code prompts elicit type hints with no eval-awareness (broad).
2. **CoT-independent RL style shift** — r260 type-hints more on the obfuscator prompts even without CoT.
3. **Genuine CoT-mediated, prompt-concentrated obfuscation** — the residual on ~22 prompts where r260
   adds silent, eval-cue-driven type-hinting above BASE. This is the real (narrow) obfuscation.
The type-hint metric is more confounded than the deconfounded gap alone suggested; the honest
RL-attributable obfuscation is the prompt-concentrated residual, measured against BASE on prefixes
where BASE stays overt (not_eval/dont_reveal/plain).
